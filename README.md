How to use:
Run the ORC.m two seperate times once for Acetone and again for Ammonia to get all figures to display.

1. It will ask you which fluid type you want to analyse 1 or 2.
2. Then it will ask for a minimum and a maximm temperature for the condenser with a step recommendation of 10.
3. Next it will ask for the same but for the evaporator temperatures.
*NOTE: The maximum Evaporator Temperature for Ammonia cannot exceed: 130 Celsius*
4. The code will generate and save a seperate file containing results.

Values I used:

For Acetone
Condenser min and max temp: 20 and 50 with a step of 10
Evaporater min and max temp: 60 and 130 with a step of 10

For Ammonia
Condenser min and max temp: 20 and 50 with a step of 10
Evaporater min and max temp: 60 and 130 with a step of 10
